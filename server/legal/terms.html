What GetYourLawyer is

GetYourLawyer is a technology platform that lets clients find independent advocates, book consultations, and follow their cases. We are not a law firm, we do not give legal advice, and we are not a party to the relationship between a client and a lawyer.

Lawyers

Lawyers are independent professionals responsible for their own advice, fees and conduct under the Advocates Act, 1961 and Bar Council rules. A lawyer is listed only after identity and Bar Council credentials are reviewed. Verification does not guarantee the outcome or quality of any service.

The assistant

The in-app assistant gives general information and routes you to a lawyer. It is not legal advice and may be incomplete or out of date.

Bookings and payments

Fees are set by lawyers and shown before you pay. Cancellations more than the stated window before a consultation are refunded in full; a lawyer's cancellation is always refunded. The platform keeps a commission from the lawyer's earnings. During the pilot, payments use a demonstration gateway and no real money is moved.

Your responsibilities

Give accurate information, keep your account secure, and do not misuse the platform, impersonate others, or upload unlawful content. We may suspend accounts that break these terms.

Emergencies

For danger to life call 112. The urgent help feature alerts lawyers but is not an emergency service.
