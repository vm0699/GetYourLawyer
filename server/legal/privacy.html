Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Draft for review. This is a starting point written for the product as built. Have a lawyer review it against the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and your data practices before launch.

What we collect

  • Account: mobile number, name, city, email (optional), date of birth and gender (optional).
  • Verification: if you use DigiLocker, we receive your name, date of birth, gender and the last 4 digits of your Aadhaar. We never see your DigiLocker password or OTP and we never store your Aadhaar number or a copy of your Aadhaar. If you upload an ID instead, the image is stored for our review team only. Lawyers also upload a Bar Council certificate.
  • Activity: bookings, case requests, case records, hearings, documents you upload, messages, invoices and payment records.
  • Location: your approximate location to show nearby lawyers. Lawyers who choose to share their live location appear on the map.
  • Device: a push notification token.

How we use it

To run the service: sign you in, verify identities, match you with lawyers, handle bookings and payments, deliver messages and reminders, prevent fraud, and meet legal obligations.

Who sees what

  • Your phone number is shared with the other side only after a booking is confirmed or a case is accepted.
  • Case documents and messages are visible only to you and your lawyer. A lawyer's private notes are never shown to the client.
  • Our verification team can view ID and Bar Council documents to review them. Each view is logged.

Retention and your choices

You can download your data or delete your account from Settings. Deleting your account erases your profile and messages. We may retain case and payment records, without your identity where possible, as required by law and for dispute resolution.

Security

Connections are encrypted. Sessions can be ended on all devices from Settings.
